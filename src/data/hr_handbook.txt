SECTION 1 — ANNUAL LEAVE & VACATION POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Entitlement
All permanent employees are entitled to 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis proportional to their contracted hours.

Leave Accrual
Annual leave accrues at a rate of 2.08 days per month of service. New employees begin accruing leave from their first day of employment. Leave may be taken in advance of full accrual with manager approval.

Requesting Leave
All vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 14 calendar days in advance. Requests are subject to manager approval and business availability. During peak business periods (Q4: October–December), a maximum of 10 consecutive days may be taken.

Carry-Over Policy
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over to the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the subsequent year or they will be forfeited. Employees leaving the company will receive payment for any unused accrued vacation days.

Public Holidays
In addition to annual leave, employees are entitled to all national public holidays. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday is observed as a substitute holiday.

SECTION 2 — REMOTE WORK POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Standard Remote Work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Eligibility requires at least 3 months of employment and a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or above. Remote work days should be agreed with your direct manager on a weekly basis.

Full Remote Work
Full remote arrangements (5 days/week) require written approval from your department head and HR. A formal Remote Work Agreement must be signed, confirming that your home workspace meets health and safety standards including adequate internet connectivity (minimum 50 Mbps), ergonomic setup, and a dedicated workspace free from distractions.

Core Hours
Regardless of location, all employees are expected to be available during core hours: 10:00–15:00 CET Monday through Friday. Meetings may be scheduled during this window without prior coordination. Outside core hours, flexible scheduling is permitted with manager notification.

Equipment for Remote Work
The company provides a laptop and necessary peripherals for remote work. Employees are responsible for maintaining equipment in good condition. A secure VPN connection is mandatory for accessing company systems remotely. VPN credentials are issued by IT on the first day.

SECTION 3 — EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Health Insurance
All employees and their immediate family members (spouse/partner and dependent children) are covered by private medical insurance from day one of employment. The plan includes: general practitioner visits, specialist consultations, hospitalization, dental care (2 cleanings per year), and vision care (annual eye exam plus glasses/contacts allowance of 200 EUR/year).

Meal Vouchers
Employees receive meal vouchers valued at 15 EUR per working day. Vouchers are loaded onto a dedicated card on the 1st of each month for the previous month's working days. They are accepted at all major supermarkets, restaurants, and food delivery platforms.

Gym & Wellness
The company reimburses gym membership or fitness activities up to 50 EUR per month. Submit receipts through the Expenses app by the 5th of the following month. Eligible activities include gym memberships, yoga, swimming, running events, and mental wellness apps.

Training Budget
Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,000 EUR for professional development. This covers online courses, conferences, certifications, and books. Requests must be approved by your manager and submitted via the HR portal. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

Equipment
A company laptop and phone (if role-relevant) are provided on day 1. Peripheral requests (monitor, keyboard, headset) are processed through the IT portal with standard delivery in 5 business days.

SECTION 4 — SICK LEAVE & MEDICAL ABSENCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Self-Certification
For sick leave of 1 to 3 consecutive working days, employees may self-certify their absence. Notify your manager and HR by 09:00 on the first day of absence via email or phone.

Medical Certificate
From day 4 of consecutive sick leave, a medical certificate from a licensed physician is required. The certificate must be submitted to HR within 24 hours of issuance. Failure to provide documentation may result in the absence being treated as unpaid leave.

Extended Sick Leave
Sick leave extending beyond 30 consecutive days transitions to the national social insurance system. HR will initiate the necessary paperwork. The company supplements the national insurance payment to maintain 80% of base salary for up to 90 days of continuous absence.

Return to Work
After extended sick leave (more than 10 days), a return-to-work meeting with HR is required. Reasonable accommodations will be made where needed, in line with applicable employment law.

SECTION 5 — WORKING HOURS & FLEXIBILITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Standard Hours
The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday through Friday, 8 hours per day excluding a 1-hour lunch break.

Flexible Start/End Times
Employees may start work between 07:00 and 10:00, and finish between 16:00 and 19:00, provided that core hours (10:00–15:00) are always covered. Flexible hours must be tracked accurately in the time management system.

Overtime
Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by your direct manager. Approved overtime is compensated at a rate of 1.5x for weekdays and 2x for weekends and public holidays. Alternatively, employees may opt for compensatory time off at the same multiplied rate. Overtime compensation must be claimed within 90 days.

Break Entitlement
Employees working more than 6 hours are entitled to a minimum 30-minute break. For shifts exceeding 9 hours, an additional 15-minute break is provided.

SECTION 6 — PAYROLL & COMPENSATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Pay Schedule
Salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of each calendar month directly to your designated bank account. If the last day falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day.

Payslips
Digital payslips are available in the HR portal under "My Documents > Payslips" on the 25th of each month. Payslips detail gross salary, deductions (tax, social insurance, health insurance contribution), net pay, and any bonuses or expense reimbursements.

Salary Reviews
Annual salary reviews take place in January following the December performance review cycle. Adjustments are effective from February 1. Market adjustments outside the annual cycle require VP-level approval.

Bonuses
Performance bonuses are paid in February based on the previous year's company performance and individual performance rating. Bonus eligibility requires completion of the full calendar year of employment.

SECTION 7 — ONBOARDING PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

First Day
On your first day, report to Reception at 09:00. You will receive your employee badge, laptop, and access credentials. An HR representative will walk you through the mandatory first-day checklist: signing employment documents, completing payroll setup, and activating your company email.

Onboarding Program
The structured onboarding program lasts 2 weeks. It includes: company overview sessions, department introductions, mandatory compliance training (GDPR, Information Security, Code of Conduct), tool access setup, and meetings with your manager and immediate team.

Buddy Program
Each new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy — a peer from your team who helps you navigate the company culture, tools, and processes during the first 30 days. Your buddy is a go-to person for informal questions.

Probation Period
The standard probation period is 3 months. Performance expectations are discussed on day 1 and reviewed at 30, 60, and 90 days with your manager. Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing by HR.

SECTION 8 — EQUIPMENT & TECHNOLOGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Standard Equipment
All employees receive: company laptop (macOS or Windows based on role), headset, mouse, and keyboard. Developers additionally receive an external monitor and optional standing desk upon request.

Equipment Requests
Additional or replacement equipment must be requested through the IT portal at it.company.com. All requests require manager approval. Standard processing time is 5 business days. Urgent requests (equipment failure) are handled within 24 hours.

Personal Use Policy
Company equipment is provided for business use. Limited personal use is permissible provided it does not interfere with work performance or violate the Acceptable Use Policy.

Equipment Return
All company equipment must be returned on the last day of employment. Failure to return equipment in working condition will result in deduction from the final paycheck equivalent to the replacement value.

Support Channels

- Slack: #it-support channel — for quick questions and status updates
- Phone: ext. 1234 — for urgent issues requiring immediate assistance
- IT Portal: it.company.com — for submitting formal tickets and tracking status

Password Resets
Password resets for company accounts can be self-served via the IT portal using your backup email. If locked out completely, call ext. 1234 for immediate assistance.

Security Incidents
Any suspected security incident (phishing, data breach, lost device) must be reported immediately to security@company.com and your manager. Do not attempt to investigate independently.

SECTION 10 — TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Learning Management System
All employees have access to the company Learning Management System (LMS) at learn.company.com. The LMS includes mandatory compliance courses, optional skill-building courses, and curated learning paths by role.

Training Budget
Each employee receives 1,000 EUR per year for external training, certifications, conferences, and books. Requests must be approved by your manager and HR at least 30 days before the event. Budget resets on January 1 and cannot be carried over.

Mandatory Training
The following courses are mandatory for all employees and must be completed within 30 days of joining: GDPR & Data Privacy, Information Security Awareness, Code of Conduct & Ethics, Health & Safety Basics. Annual refreshers are required for all mandatory courses.

Mentoring Program
The company runs a voluntary mentoring program matching junior employees with senior professionals across departments. Applications open in March and September. Contact HR at hr@company.com to apply as a mentor or mentee.

SECTION 11 — PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year:
- Mid-year Review (June): focuses on progress against goals, development areas, and support needed for H2.
- Annual Review (December): full assessment of performance and goal achievement. Results feed into salary review and bonus decisions.

Rating Scale
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory. Ratings at "Needs Improvement" or below trigger a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

Goal Setting
Individual goals are set at the start of each year in collaboration with the manager, aligned to team and company objectives. Goals should follow the SMART framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

360-Degree Feedback
The annual review includes 360-degree feedback from peers, direct reports, and stakeholders. Each employee selects 3–5 feedback providers from a pre-approved list. Feedback is anonymous and summarized by HR.

SECTION 12 — PARKING & TRANSPORTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Office Parking
Parking is available at the office building on a first-come, first-served basis. To request a reserved parking spot, submit a request to Facilities via facilities@company.com. Reserved spots are allocated based on business need and seniority. Monthly parking fee for reserved spots: 30 EUR, deducted from payroll.

Public Transport Subsidy
The company subsidizes public transport passes at 50% of the monthly cost. Submit your transport pass invoice to HR by the 5th of each month for reimbursement in that month's payroll.

Electric Vehicle Charging
EV charging stations are available in the underground car park (Level B1, spaces 1–10). Charging is provided free of charge for employees during office hours.

Bicycle Policy
Secure bicycle storage and shower facilities are available on-site. Employees commuting by bicycle are eligible for the Cycling Allowance — 0.25 EUR per km commuted, up to 50 EUR/month.

SECTION 13 — EXPENSE REPORTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Eligible Expenses
Business expenses eligible for reimbursement include: client entertainment, business travel (flights, hotels, ground transport), conference registration fees, team events approved by management, and business meals.

Submission Process
All expenses must be submitted through the Expenses app within 30 days of incurring the expense. Receipts are mandatory for any individual item over 25 EUR. Group expenses require a list of attendees and business purpose.

Approval & Reimbursement
Expenses are approved by your direct manager within 5 business days of submission. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the following month's payroll. Expense claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed unless exceptional circumstances are documented.

Travel Policy
Business travel must be pre-approved by your manager for domestic trips and by your department head for international travel. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval. Hotel bookings should not exceed 150 EUR/night without prior approval.
